## Not built

- **Pop-out output window:** it still doesn't show the Clip Auto-Editor. Use F or Ctrl+Space for a clean screen. *(Known since the alpha.)*
- **Pre-roll in live playback:** before the control track starts, the cuts are made by the fast pass and are checked in the export. Playing that stretch live with no music hasn't been tested on its own.
- **Code signing:** not done, so Windows still shows "Run anyway".

## Differences from the alpha you'll notice

- **Cuts follow one beat grid from 0:00** in track mode, found when the song loads. Before, they followed the realtime beat detector. TEMPO fixes a wrong tempo (÷2, ×2, type, tap), and Live clock gives the old behaviour.
- **Unplayed parts of the song** export with motion and cuts, not a still picture.
- **Images play Over video by default** in new projects. Alpha images keep Full frame.
- **Timeline:**
  - Click a lane to jump.
  - Drag on the ruler to select a range (it used to jump). L loops it, and Export can render just the selection.
  - Double-clicking the Master lane picks up the control track; double-clicking elsewhere still fits the view.
- **Bottom shelf:**
  - The lanes are drawers on the dark Master background, and the main divider resizes them all.
  - The clip lane is a compact band strip; ▸ shows the full lane.
  - The label column is wider (136 px).
- **Master header:** Start [0:00] · music length on one line, then the source with its activity light. Times read m:ss.
- **Action clips:** cards are coloured by band. A clip's sections show as a strip on its one card, which grows when there are many. Two players at most per video.
- **Shelves (left and right)** are laid out like 01 File.
- **Saving:** projects saved in Stable05 don't open in the alpha.
